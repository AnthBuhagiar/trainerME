How it works

An estimate you can trust, a plan you can read.

The engine estimates hour power from your rides. The rulebook turns that number, your goal and your available days into a week of training.

The hour-power engine

From everyday rides to one number.

Three signals combine into one estimate, adjusted toward what you could really hold for 60 minutes.

Signal 1

Critical-power curve

Best 3–30 minute efforts from any ride, fitted to a two-parameter curve. Shorter sprints are ignored.

Signal 2

Heart-rate efficiency

Watts at a fixed heart rate on steady riding, compared with your own history.

Signal 3

Durability

How well your power holds in the last hour of long rides.

Output

A confidence range

Fresh evidence narrows the range; time without it widens it.

Building the plan

P1No more than two interval sessions a week.
P2At least 70% easy in base, 75% later; no more than 15% at threshold or above.
P6–7About 10% progression a week; every fourth week at about 70% volume.
P8No back-to-back hard days.
P11Two-week taper before your event, to about 60% then 45%.
P12Life first: your chosen days or usual week. Missed sessions are never carried over.

Adapting to what you actually did

P13A week or more off brings an ease-back or comeback week.
P14Progression only counts if you completed at least 70% of sessions.
P16Hour power down 3% over three weeks despite good adherence adds a recovery week.
P18Runs, rows and swims of 30+ minutes count for easy days, never key sessions.
P17–20A hard ride or other hard session yesterday makes today easy; no strength two days running.

Ethics and data policy

Your rides are yours.

A feature that breaks a principle does not ship. The policy is reviewed yearly and whenever scope changes.

Read the privacy policy
Principle

You own your data

See, export, correct or delete it at any time.

Principle

Collect the minimum

Only what the estimate and plan need.

Principle

Honest about uncertainty

Ranges, not false precision.

Principle

You stay in control

Accept or decline new zones. Your chosen days come first.

Principle

Health before performance

Rules cap intensity and build in recovery.

Principle

Fair to every rider

Nothing hard-coded to one athlete.

What we will never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your data
  • Use it for advertising or profiling
  • Show it to another rider without your opt-in
  • Send it to AI or outside services to make plans
  • Train machine-learning models on it
  • Publish your routes or start points
  • Keep it after you delete your account or revoke Strava
  • Publish accuracy results unless anonymised and aggregated

Data we handle

  • Strava ID, name and access tokens
  • Rides: power, heart rate, duration, distance, type
  • Other activities of 30+ minutes
  • Goals, GPX routes, finish-time and fuel targets
  • Training, away and time-off days
  • Daily check-ins
  • Referral codes, and subscription periods once billing opens
  • Live trainer and heart-rate data, which stays in your browser

Heart rate, check-ins and wellbeing notes are treated as sensitive health information.

Your rights, self-serve

  • See everything held about you
  • Export: backup, .zwo, .erg, .ics, Intervals.icu
  • Correct anything
  • Decline any new estimate
  • Delete your account in the app
  • Revoke access in Strava; we delete automatically
  • Contact us and get a reply within 7 days

Who else is involved

  • Strava — required, to read your rides
  • Intervals.icu — optional, for Garmin and Zwift export
  • DigitalOcean, Cloudflare — hosting only
  • GitHub — code only, never rider data

A billing provider will be added to the policy before subscriptions open, as will any new integration.

If something goes wrong

Contain→Assess→Notify riders within 72 h→Report to OAIC if required→Fix and record

Leaving the beta deletes your data, and no one is quoted without permission.

Not a medical device or a coach.

Speak to a doctor before starting structured training if you have any health risks. Stop riding and get help if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.
